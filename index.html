Restore, colorize, and animate faded family photos with AI. Bring treasured memories back to life in seconds.

AI Photo Enhancement

A multi-model AI pipeline removes noise, sharpens faces, and heals scratches on old prints. Process any photo from your library or scan a physical print directly with your camera.

Realistic Colorization

Turn black-and-white photos into full color with natural, realistic hues. Revivo analyzes context to pick era-appropriate tones for skin, clothing, and backgrounds.

Photo Animation

See a still portrait move again. Revivo animates faces and scenes into short, lifelike clips — ideal for anniversaries, tributes, or simply watching grandparents smile.

Heritage Albums

Organize restored photos into themed albums with AI-generated captions. Export as beautiful PDF photo books to print, share, or preserve for generations.

Private by Design

Your photos stay yours. Revivo doesn't train on your uploads, never sells your data, and gives you full control to delete everything at any time.
